Rejected transfer

Useful rules do not travel cleanly.

The same relative growth-rule mutation was beneficial in two of eight species, neutral in one, and nonviable in five even without injury. This rejects universal transfer for this mutation in the tested catalogue. The original screen reused phases and rotations; its trials are not independent replicates. Inspect the species record →

002

Magnitude holdout / candidate 806

The cliff moved.
It did not disappear.

A new candidate selected on 4%, 6% and 8% injuries passed the recovery endpoint in 32/32 held-out contexts at both 5% and 7%, then 0/32 at 10% and 1/32 at 12%. These are eight independent noisy initial-condition seeds, each measured at four phases. Its 50.8% mean missed the 65% gate. It is distinct from Genesis 001 and was not promoted. 003

Charter gate 3 / coverage passed

More diversity, unevenly.

Across eight species and 20 search seeds per species, MAP-Elites occupied 0.78 more behavior bins than random search on average (95% seed-bootstrap interval: 0.58–0.99), at equal simulation compute. Orbium favored random search. This passes the aggregate coverage gate; whether diversity improves recovery is a separate comparison. Inspect the coverage record → Read the locked protocol →

Species still mattered.

At 64 evaluations per search seed, direct regeneration produced 48.4% successful selections, MAP-Elites 40.6%, and performance selection 32.4%. Success requires a valid undamaged control and functional recovery. The result combines stability and recovery, varies by species, and does not establish broad superiority. Compare the species ↓

004

Legacy screen / Genesis 001

A narrow central-injury advantage.

The earlier two-parameter Orbium mutation survived every tested 5% central injury and failed at 7.5%. That screen measured survival-related endpoints and reused near-symmetric rotations. It motivated the stronger matched-control shape and recovery-time tests above; it does not establish broad regeneration.

005

Ecological threshold

Proximity became the predator.

Under the strongest symmetric pressure, coexistence fell from 84.7% when organisms began near one another to 8.3% when their bodies overlapped. A separate held-out run reproduced the same direction: 86.8% versus 25.7%.

006

Failed promotion

The promising advantage got smaller.

Genesis 001 appeared 7.5 points better than its parent during discovery. In 252 unseen matched contexts the difference contracted to 2.0 points and missed the precommitted gate. It remains an observation, not a promoted finding.

007

Evolution run 001

Coexistence was not four numbers away.

Of 2,048 paired growth-rule mutations, 77 preserved both parental identities. The best honest candidate rescued 2 of 8 unseen hard encounters from a zero baseline, but passed only 1 of 4 training contexts. Local improvement appeared; robust co-adaptation did not.

008

Evolution run 002

Memory transferred two encounters.

With both bodies fixed, the best training-selected interaction genome improved unseen coexistence from 1 of 8 encounters to 3 of 8. Both added survivals occurred at contact; overlap stayed at 1 of 4. Remembered boundaries mattered, but robust cooperation did not emerge.
